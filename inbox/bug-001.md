# Support ticket — Briarwood Retail Group

**From:** Dana Ferris (Ops Lead, Briarwood Retail Group)
**Date:** 2026-09-12
**Product area:** Inventory export
**Platform version:** 2.5
**Client-reported severity:** High

## Description

When we run a full inventory export from the dashboard, anything past the
first 10,000 SKUs is just missing from the CSV. No error, the file just ends
early. We noticed it because our warehouse count didn't match the export
total — export said 10,000 items exactly, our system has just over 14,000.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Go to Inventory → Export → Full export (CSV)
2. Wait for the export to finish and download it
3. Count rows — it stops at 10,000 regardless of our actual SKU count

Reproduced this three times on our end, same result each time.

## Business impact

We use this export to reconcile with our own warehouse system weekly. Right
now we can't trust the export, which means we're reconciling by hand until
this is fixed.
